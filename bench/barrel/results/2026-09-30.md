## Judged (0-10; weights: modelling 35%, UVs 20%, texturing 35%, process 10%)

Judged by Opus 5.5 (the session that wrote the benchmark), which is also a contestant: treat the

**Opus 5.5.** Cleanest topology of the field: gridded head boards and saddles, no sliver fans. The
bung is cut through the stave, and the saddles are fitted to the barrel's curve with 1.5 mm
clearance. There is a stretcher under the spout. The brand is the strongest: "OLD OAK BREWERY · XXX ·
EST. 1872", rendered from Blender text into an image and applied as a Patina decal. Weak spots: the
tap handle is too golden and clean, and the holder dirt reads as soot.

**GPT 6.1 Sol.** The most polished texturing: crisp stylized grain on every board, a drip tray with a
readable beer puddle, a "HEARTH & HOPS" brand with a hop emblem, a curved faucet spout and bolt heads
on the cradle. It was methodical, writing its own UV audit/repair and delivery verification. Weak
spots: fan-triangulated heads and saddles, and hoop UVs laid out as rings.

**GPT 6 Astra.** Design close to Sol's (curved faucet, drip tray, pegged cradle, brand roundel). It
has the densest UV packing, with aligned grain and a good brass tap. Weak spots: fan-triangulated
heads and saddles, a subtle puddle, and it was the slowest.

**Sonnet 5.5.** The leanest mesh at 16.6k triangles, with efficient large stave islands, a slatted
drip tray and a hop-cone brand. Weak spots: the tap reads as a brass block with no clear spout,
long fan triangles on the saddles and heads, a subtle wet zone, and wood that is a little noisy.

## Observations

- Every contestant cleared every hard requirement. The benchmark discriminates on judgment,
  topology and texturing, not on validity.
- Both GPT models converged on nearly the same design language.
- Three of four fan-triangulated large flat n-gons. The wireframe view is what exposes it; the lit
  renders do not.
