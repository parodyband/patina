# Reference-match barrel benchmark, 2026-09-30

Brief: `bench/barrel_ref/TASK.md` with `reference.png` and the stylized-environment-art skill. The task
is to sculpt a high poly in headless Blender, build a quad-based low poly of 8k triangles or fewer, use
one material with maximized UVs, bake in Patina and texture to match. Patina 0.3.3. Codex ran at extra
high effort with the fast service tier (accepted by the CLI; the tier is not echoed in its output). All
four contestants ran in parallel on one machine. Renders, wireframes, baked normals and UV layouts are
in `C:\dev\patina-bench\2026-09-30-ref\results\`.

- **Opus 5.5.** It has the reference's heavy dark hoops and the closest proportions. There is a
  sculpted crack on the front stave, as in the reference. The low poly is textbook: 4k triangles,
  0.2% slivers and buried faces deleted, over a 3.2M-triangle sculpt. Its UV coverage is lower (66%).
- **Fable 5.1.** (added 2026-10-01; it ran alone, so its time is not comparable.) It is the best
  measured game asset of the round: the lightest low poly, its own MaxRects packer giving 75.5%
  coverage with perfectly even density (1.00) and no mirrored islands, and the fewest real bake
  misses. Its likeness drifts: many narrow staves, thin hoops, upright legs with crossbars instead of
  the splayed trestle, and a small tap.
- **GPT 6 Astra.** Its lumpy wooden tap and stave ends are nearly a copy of the reference, but the
  hoops are thin. It modelled chamfers into the low poly, which gives the most slivers, and it has
  many mirrored islands.
- **Sonnet 5.5.** It was the fastest and has a clean 4k low poly with no mirrored islands and 75%
  coverage. The hoops are overweight, there are only 8 staves, and the tap is a plain T.
- **GPT 6.1 Sol.** It has the best UVs (77% coverage, 1.04 spread). The stand runs tall, and the head
  rim carries a dotted highlight.

The previous round's entries were 36-49% sliver triangles. With the bevels moved into the sculpt and
bake, every entry here is under 9%.
